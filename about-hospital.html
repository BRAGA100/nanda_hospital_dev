About nanda hospital

Advance Endo-Laparoscopy & Gyneac Centre

Nanda Hospital - Advance Endo-Laparoscopy & Gyneac Surgery Centre is a multispeciality hospital, 25 bedded hospital, situated near ambedkar chowk & in the centre of the city.

Advance Endo-Laparoscopy & Gyneac Centre ensure our patients achieve a high level of healthcare with as much comfort as possible. We believe in making quality tertiary healthcare services accessible and equitable to all. nanda hospital is driven by the dedicated and well-trained team, whose core values is to provide healthcare with compassion. Our commitment towards ethical practices & excellence in the healthcare services has ensured that best healthcare at an affordable price is provided to those who are in need.

Our Mission, Vision & Aim

Our Mission

To set the benchmark in healthcare by delivering world-class preventive, curative, and holistic medical services that are safe, ethical, and accessible to all. We strive to touch lives across every section of society — with compassion, innovation, and affordability at the core of our care. Through strategic goals and dynamic outreach programs, we empower communities with knowledge, awareness, and proactive health engagement.

Our Vision

At Nanda Hospital, we are committed to delivering cutting-edge diagnostic and therapeutic services using state-of-the-art technology — all within a world-class environment. Our care is driven by passion, guided by precision, and centered around patient well-being.

Our Aim

At Nanda Hospital, we are devoted to treating every patient with dignity and respect. We strive to foster a transparent, compassionate, and patient-friendly environment. Our foundation rests on the triad of Integrity, Sensitivity, and Trust — values that guide every step of our care journey.
